# Design notebook

The morning run's memory for craft, kept short on purpose (60 lines at most; drop
the oldest lines first). One or two lines per issue: what was tried, what worked,
what to avoid next time. Facts about stories never go here.

- 2026-10-14 No. 1: medium-blue + fluoro-pink on bright-white, poster type, split hero with generated art. Fourteen blocks felt full; twelve is probably the sweet spot. Stat blocks in a third column need short values (under 6 characters). A wide chart with only two bars reads thin; give charts four or more bars or use a stat instead.
- 2026-10-15 No. 2: teal + coral on fog, federal-blue text, wide type, stack hero with drawn art. Wide type makes big titles very tall: keep hero titles to about 30 characters and avoid long single words (phones clip them). A cycle diagram with 6 nodes collides with its labels; use flow for 5+ nodes. Three stats in a half block overflow in wide type; two is the limit. Before quoting a source, check the last issues: yesterday's quote source came back today and validate caught it.
- Reader feedback 2026-10-15: decorative visuals (generated shapes, symbolic drawn art) add nothing; diagrams, data charts and real images work. The kit no longer generates art, and drawn art must be a labelled schematic.
- Kit fix 2026-10-15: headline sizes now scale with the day's typeface (wide 0.72, grotesk 0.9, poster and condensed 1) and the hero title is smaller overall; a wide-type hero should now fit in two or three lines.
- Reader feedback 2026-10-15: make it digestible for curious non-specialists. Every block now has a plain gist, defined terms, a kind and level; details fold into deep; the grid is split into themed groups; Learn one thing explains a concept from zero. No. 1 and No. 2 were rewritten this way.
- 2026-10-16 No. 3: purple + orange on butter, black text, grotesk type, split hero with a real architecture figure (contain) from the launch post. Practitioner blocks fold to a stub, so a folded list or steps beside a tall block leaves a hole; keep practitioner level for blocks that sit alone or beside short ones. Product screenshots that do not show the story (a launcher library grid on an anti-cheat story) read as decoration; drop them.
- 2026-10-17 No. 4: bright-red + cornflower on bright-white, federal-blue text, condensed type, stack hero with a 6-node kit flow diagram (reads well, stacks cleanly on phones). The poster variant with no image let the title ride over the lane label; use stack or split for diagram heroes. A full-width chart image inside a compare block swamped it and repeated the rows; let the rows carry the numbers. `tryIt` renders as plain code, so no backticks or Markdown links in it.
- 2026-10-18 No. 5: blue + sunflower on blush, brown text, grotesk type, split hero with a kit flow diagram. In split, the diagram stacks vertically; six nodes left a tall hole beside the title, four fit. A practitioner (folded) block beside a tall spotlight left a hole again: keep folded blocks next to short ones or unfold them. A tall image concept pairs best with a tall steps block in two halves; put the short timeline third beside a wide spotlight.
- 2026-10-19 No. 6: teal + orange on lilac, black text, poster type, stack hero with a 5-node kit flow diagram. A paper figure in a poster hero got cropped to its top half and the title ran over its credit line; tall figures need split or a diagram instead. A stat with an image in a third beside a wide spotlight left a big hole; half and half with a trimmed stat body fits. Validate wants 4 visual blocks: look for article figures (InfoQ diagrams, store screenshots) before settling.
- 2026-10-20 No. 7: green + fluoro-pink on fog, federal-blue text, grotesk type, split hero with the source's own cost chart (contain); a vendor chart with annotated changes reads better than any diagram we could draw. A wide stat beside long steps left a hole; wide steps (image on top) plus a third stat fits. Hub diagrams stack left spokes then right spokes in narrow columns, so order nodes as 1,3,5 then 2,4 for a readable top-to-bottom list. Check the last week of tickers and Try this before picking stories: Godot 4.8 beta 1, the Kukshtel harness post and the OpenCode breach had all run already.
- 2026-10-21 No. 8: medium-blue + coral on mint, black text, condensed type, stack hero with a kit diagram. A hub diagram in a stack hero squeezes into the left half with tall narrow boxes; a 5-row layers diagram fills the band and reads as a checklist. A tall steps block with an image beside a third timeline left a 900px hole; give image steps the full row. Keep the hero title different from the issue title, or the page repeats itself at the top.
- 2026-10-22 No. 9: purple + aqua on bright-white, federal-blue text, wide type, split hero with the paper's own overview figure (contain); a dense figure that already explains the pipeline beats any kit diagram. Wide type makes a 70-character issue title run five lines; keep it near 35. A concept with an image beside a third stat left a hole; pair image blocks half and half and trim points, and drop a spotlight's tryIt when it only repeats the source URL.
- 2026-10-23 No. 10: green + orange on sky, burgundy text, poster type, stack hero with a 5-node kit flow diagram (the source's own hero art was decorative, so it was dropped).
