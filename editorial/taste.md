# Reader profile

Radar is written for one developer first. Anyone else who reads it is welcome, but
every editorial choice is made for this reader. Tune this file freely; the triage
step and the morning run both read it.

## Who

- An independent developer and studio founder (Imperion Games, based in Portugal).
- Builds modular Unreal Engine 5 C++ gameplay plugins sold on Fab (combat, inventory,
  interaction, world tools) and an action RPG that uses them.
- Also builds browser and mobile games (web tech, three.js), SaaS products
  (Node, React, Supabase) and Flutter apps.
- Works AI-first: Claude Code every day, with subagents, skills, hooks, MCP servers,
  routines and long-running automations. Cares about how to work faster and better
  with agents, not about AI in the abstract.

## Lanes

1. **Game development**: engines and their releases (Unreal first, then Unity, Godot
   and others), tools and middleware, rendering and tech, AI in game production,
   marketplaces and stores (Fab, Steam, mobile stores, web portals), the business of
   small studios, postmortems and talks with real lessons.
2. **AI engineering and software workflows**: coding agents and their releases
   (Claude Code first), models that change what agents can do, techniques people are
   adopting, MCP and agent tooling, evals, pricing and limits, security of agents,
   practitioners writing from real experience.

## What earns a place

- It changes how this reader works this week: a release, a setting, a technique,
  a price change, a new tool worth trying.
- It shifts the ground under their products: engine direction, store policy,
  market data, what buyers of plugins and tools will expect.
- It teaches something reusable: a pattern, a postmortem, a benchmark with method.
- Primary sources and people who show their work beat commentary.
- Numbers with context beat adjectives.

## What does not

- Consumer game news: trailers, reviews, sales charts, console wars, unless there is
  a development lesson in it.
- Gadget and consumer AI app launches with no developer angle.
- Funding rounds and acquisitions on their own; only as evidence of a trend.
- Opinion pieces with no new evidence; AI doom or hype in either direction.
- Crypto, NFTs, politics, celebrity CEOs, unless a decision directly changes the work.
- Anything older than three days, unless it was missed and still matters.

## Tone

Developer to developer. Plain words, short sentences, concrete nouns. Explain what it
is, what changed, and what to do about it. No hype words: powerful, seamless, robust,
game-changing, revolutionary, unlock, supercharge.
